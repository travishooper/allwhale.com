KidFriendly Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

KidFriendly (“the app”, “we”, “us”) helps parents and caregivers discover kid-friendly restaurants, parks, and recreation nearby. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have — including how to delete your account and data.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account we store your email address, an optional display name, and a securely hashed version of your password. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Location. With your permission, the app uses your device location to center the map and show places near you. Your precise location is used on-device to request nearby places and is not stored as a location history on our servers.
  • Your contributions. Places you favorite, attributes you confirm or deny (e.g. “has high chairs”), and “not kid friendly” flags you submit are saved to your account so the community sees accurate information.
  • Technical data. Standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) are processed to operate the service and prevent abuse.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: sign-in, showing nearby places, and saving your favorites and contributions.
  • To improve the accuracy of place information through community input.
  • To secure the service, debug issues, and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Google Maps & Places. The app displays maps using Google Maps, and place listings are sourced from the Google Places API (requested by our server). Your use of Google features is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting. Our API and database are hosted on cloud infrastructure (Hetzner). Data is stored on servers we control.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time:

Delete your account: allwhale.com/kidfriendly/delete

When you delete your account, we permanently remove your login, favorites, votes, and flags. Contributions that improve place data for everyone (such as a confirmed “has changing tables”) are retained in anonymized form, no longer linked to you. Routine backups are purged on a rolling schedule.

Children’s privacy

KidFriendly is intended for parents and caregivers (adults). It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are hashed, traffic is served over HTTPS, and access to the database is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
